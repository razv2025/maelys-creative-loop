Creative Loop · Data6
How performance shapes the next creative

Four concrete feedback mechanisms

1 · Learnings in the prompt

The brief generator is prompted with the live learning table — "creator-confession hooks +35% ROAS." Every concept must cite a learning, or declare itself an exploration.

2 · Explore / exploit budget

70% iterate winners · 20% new hooks on proven angles · 10% swings. Enforced by the concept queue itself.

3 · Fatigue triggers refresh

CTR decay per angle, not per ad, auto-triggers refresh briefs — before decay reaches ROAS.

4 · Optimize to LTV, not first purchase

Winners re-ranked on 90-day LTV:CAC and refund rate — a "$0" frame can win CVR while leaking into returns.

Implementation prioritization

Learn from history first. Generate second. Automate last.

Sequenced by risk: each phase pays for itself before the next adds autonomy.

Retroactive intelligence

  • Genome auto-tagger on the historical library
  • Join tags ↔ Meta · GA4 · LTV · A/B archive
  • First weekly intelligence report

Zero workflow change. Insight from money already spent.

Walk · weeks 6–16

Learning-grounded generation

  • Brief & script generator, learnings in context
  • Ad→LP builder in production (Part 2)
  • Veo teaser pretests
  • Human approval gates on everything

Velocity ×3–5 at constant headcount.

Run · week 16+

Closed-loop automation

  • Launch/test/retire rules via Meta API
  • Bandit-style explore/exploit allocation
  • LTV-objective optimization · LP personalization

The loop runs nightly.

Creative Loop · First builds9
KPIs — and how each is actually measured

Measure the engine, the business, and the failure modes

Engine health
  • Creative hit rate — % beating portfolio-median ROAS at the test gate
  • Concept→live time — queue to first impression, in days
  • Learning velocity — validated learnings per month
  • Cost per winning creative — test spend ÷ winners
Business
  • Blended CAC & ROAS — Meta cross-checked vs GA4 + first-party
  • 90-day LTV:CAC by creative cohort — the scaling decision
  • New-creative share of spend — aging-winner risk
  • ATC & LP CVR per archetype — GA4, per generated page
Guardrails
  • Refund rate per offer framing — "$0" must not buy CVR with returns
  • Fatigue index — CTR decay slope per angle
  • Compliance flag rate — 0 unhedged claims shipped
  • Brand consistency — weekly sampled human QA

Part 2 · Working prototype

I built the loop — it runs today, and it shows its sources

Three stages, all live: ad in → analysis → landing page → a new AI-generated ad. Every listed data source consumed — and labeled where it's used.

1 · Ad Intelligence

Gemini watches the full video and returns its creative DNA — hook, angle, persona, claims, compliance flags, recommended archetype.

2 · Hook-matched landing page

Your real archetypes, voice, offer and live CDN imagery (likeness-gated) + optional free-text direction. Every section carries a provenance chip.

Data as fuel, not scoreboard

Fixed internal dataset + 4 competitor funnels scraped live — cited sources with clickable links. The new page's own results are unknown until it runs.

3 · The loop closes — with Veo

Learnings + competitor gaps → three concepts (safe / new-hook / swing) → one click renders a real 8-second Veo ad.

What I found in your live funnel (20 Aug 2026)

Six findings from the GET-DREAMY teardown

Hook-match decays after the hero

The Caroline advertorial = the generic page except H1 + banner. Zero Caroline below the fold.

Speed leaks CVR before copy matters

Synchronous VWO (2s) · 12 autoplay videos · no lazy-loading on the flagship template.

Three prices in one viewport

"$5" badge, "$0" button, "$43.20 $54" sub-line — dissonance at the decision moment.

Trust numbers disagree

1M vs 2M jars · 30/56 vs 33/28-day trial footnotes · a countdown hardcoded to 15:06.

Friction at the ATC moment

A forced unselected radio in the offer widget; no sticky ATC on the long advertorials.

The quiz forgets its promise

Pledges a "$35 mystery gift," then never mentions it — and ignores the user's answers.
